Lemon Blueberry Cupcakes!

I created this recipe myself!
Tried-and-true food chemistry combined with loads of grocery store trips for more flour. Working on this recipe took many batches.
my goal for this recipe was to make it easy to get good, tall cupcakes. usually a beginner cupcake falls flat for many reasons so you need to know EVERYTHING about baking!

  • Time:
  • Serves: 12 cupcakes
Dry Ingredients Quantity
all-purpose flour 1 1/2 cups
baking powder 2 teaspoons
granulated sugar 3/4 cup
kosher salt 1/2 teaspoon
Wet Ingredients Quantity
vanilla extract 1 tablespoon
Eggs 2 eggs
sour cream 1/2 cup
vegetable oil 1/2 cup
frozen blueberries 1 cup approx.

substitutes:

sour cream - buttermilk or greek yogurt

Step 1: In a large bowl, sift flour, baking powder, and salt.
Step 2: in a smaller bowl, whisk eggs, oil, sour cream, and vanilla
Step 3: add your wet ingredients to the dry mixture and mix until JUST combined. (overmixing leads to air bubbles)
Step 4 coat FROZEN blueberries in some flour, just enough to lightly coat them. it is the blueberries' protection from the hot oven!
Shake off excess flour as it can affect how the cupcakes bake.
Step 5: line muffin pan and add 1/2 of the batter to each liner
Bake on 350 degrees (Fahrenheit please) for 18-24 minutes. If a toothpick comes out with just a few moist crumbs, take them out and let them cool!
